## Features
* **Multi-Zone Configuration:** Define granular risk percentages, level counts, and take-profit targets across up to 10 distinct market zones.
* **Triangular Exposure Model:** Automatically calculates cumulative total risk relative to market depth and adverse movement.
* **Built-in Strategy Presets:** Quickly test popular risk profiles including *Scalping*, *DCA*, *Aggressive*, *Moderate*, *Long Life*, *Gold Special*, and *YOLO*.
* **Recovery Calculator:** Computes the exact number of consecutive winning trades required at a selected zone TP to recover a full-loss scenario.
* **Expected Value (EV) Simulator:** Interactive probability slider tool to gauge net expected value per trade under varying market conditions.
