Total Risk uses the EA’s distance-based model (matches lot sizing):
At the end of level L’s band (depth DL): Total Risk = Σ Riski × (DL − Pi) ÷ Spacei
When all zones share the same Space, this equals the triangular series (1, 3, 6, 10…).

Recovery from 1 Full Loss
Trades needed = Max Total Risk ÷ selected Zone TP (rounded up).
This is the number of consecutive wins at that TP required to recover one full loss to the final SL.
